The Android app for AutoFileSync, the free program for Windows, macOS and Linux that keeps files in two places the same.

<b>How it works</b>
<ul>
<li>In AutoFileSync on the PC, choose <i>File → Connect phone…</i>.</li>
<li>Scan the QR code with the app and tap “Connect”.</li>
<li>On the PC, choose what should happen: back up photos and videos, keep a folder the same on both devices, or bring music to the phone.</li>
</ul>

From then on the phone synchronises by itself: every 30 minutes, shortly after new photos and at the tap of a button – optionally only on Wi-Fi and only while charging. Everything is set up on the PC.

<b>Safe</b>
<ul>
<li>PC and phone check each other with certificates; the QR code is valid for ten minutes and only once.</li>
<li>The file server on the phone is open only during a synchronisation, with a one-time password.</li>
<li>No cloud service, no account: the data only travels within your own network from the phone to the PC.</li>
</ul>

The app needs access to all files so that the PC can read and write the folders that were set up.
